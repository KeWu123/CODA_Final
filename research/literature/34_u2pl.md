# Semi-Supervised Semantic Segmentation Using Unreliable Pseudo-Labels (U2PL)

- Authors: Yuchao Wang et al.
- Venue: CVPR 2022
- URL: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Wang_Semi-Supervised_Semantic_Segmentation_Using_Unreliable_Pseudo-Labels_CVPR_2022_paper.html

## Key finding

U2PL separates reliable from unreliable pixels using prediction entropy and uses the
unreliable pixels as class-wise negative evidence instead of imposing their most likely
class as hard positive supervision.

## Relevance to OBA

OBA continues to apply an unfiltered LCC hard pseudo-label to both transformed views.
Antithetic sampling cannot cancel shared pseudo-label error, so the progressive
high-weight collapse is consistent with a known confirmation-bias failure mode. Adding
U2PL-style uncertainty machinery would violate the single-core constraint and is not
authorized as an OBA rescue.
