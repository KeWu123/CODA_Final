# Conformal Data Augmentation

- Paper: *Filtering with Confidence: When Data Augmentation Meets Conformal Prediction*.
- Status/year: ICLR 2026 submission, desk rejected; not an accepted top-conference result.
- Core idea: conformal filtering of synthetic samples with risk control in classification and tabular tasks.
- Relevance: adjacent evidence that augmentation validity can be treated as risk control rather than heuristic strength.
- Difference: no semi-supervised dense prediction, no teacher/student view selection, and no medical segmentation.
- Important caution: do not claim ViSA-MT is the first risk-controlled augmentation method in general.
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21479
- OpenReview: https://openreview.net/forum?id=GuncMyZ3uN
