# MatchMask / MatchMask++

- Venue/year: CVPR 2026.
- Core idea: mask-centric diffusion augmentation adapted with a small layer-targeted adapter and filtering; MatchMask++ extends to semi-supervised segmentation.
- Relevance: generative augmentation is now a strong, current baseline in label-scarce segmentation.
- Difference: ViSA-MT does not synthesize new images with a diffusion model and targets medical weak-to-strong view selection.
- Paper: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Lin_MatchMask_Mask-Centric_Generative_Data_Augmentation_for_Label-Scarce_Semantic_Segmentation_CVPR_2026_paper.html
