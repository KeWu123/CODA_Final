# BoundaryMix

- Primary source: [Pattern Recognition 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2021.107924)
- Core: under scribble supervision, identifies risky predicted-boundary regions,
  removes/replaces pixels from another image, and mixes pseudo-labels.
- Collision: occupies Cartesian boundary-region replacement and the name
  “BoundaryMix.”
- BMER distinction: no region cutting/pasting, no label mixing, and no change to spatial
  semantics; only an empirical profile field is resynthesized in recipient coordinates.
