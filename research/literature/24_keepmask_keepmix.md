# KeepMask / KeepMix

- Primary source: [Image and Vision Computing 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0262885624001604)
- Core: simple medical segmentation augmentation that protects or composes foreground
  regions while varying background/context.
- Collision: rules out broad claims that preserving anatomy/foreground under local
  appearance augmentation is new.
- BMER distinction: BMER deliberately resynthesizes the *two-sided interface evidence*
  in normal coordinates; it does not protect the full foreground or paste an organ.
