# beta-FFT

- Primary source: [CVPR 2025 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Hu_beta-FFT_Nonlinear_Interpolation_and_Differentiated_Training_Strategies_for_Semi-Supervised_Medical_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf)
- Core: nonlinear interpolation of exchanged low-frequency Fourier components plus
  differentiated co-training; evaluates PROMISE12.
- Collision: makes generic frequency/style exchange a crowded main direction.
- BMER distinction: models conditional interface evidence in object-relative
  coordinates instead of marginal whole-image frequency statistics.
