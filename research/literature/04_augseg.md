# AugSeg

- Paper: *Augmentation Matters: A Simple-Yet-Effective Approach to Semi-Supervised Semantic Segmentation*.
- Venue/year: CVPR 2023.
- Core idea: continuously sampled intensity distortions plus confidence-adaptive labeled-information injection.
- Relevance: closest top-conference precedent for augmentation-centered SSL.
- Gap relative to ViSA-MT: uses confidence to adapt augmentation/mixing but does not explicitly test whether a candidate transform is teacher-stable and student-challenging.
- Paper: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhao_Augmentation_Matters_A_Simple-Yet-Effective_Approach_to_Semi-Supervised_Semantic_Segmentation_CVPR_2023_paper.html
- Code: https://github.com/ZhenZHAO/AugSeg
