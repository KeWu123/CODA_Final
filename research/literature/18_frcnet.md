# FRCNet

- Paper: *Frequency and Region Consistency for Semi-supervised Medical Image Segmentation*.
- Venue/year: MICCAI 2024.
- Core idea: frequency-domain consistency and multi-granularity region similarity consistency.
- Relevance: frequency-aware consistency is established; wavelet/Fourier decomposition alone cannot carry a new paper.
- Gap relative to ViSA-MT: candidate selection in the validity-utility plane is orthogonal and could use frequency transforms only as members of a bank, not as the contribution.
