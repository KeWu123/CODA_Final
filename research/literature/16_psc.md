# Pair Shuffle Consistency (PSC)

- Venue/year: MICCAI 2024.
- Core idea: split paired labeled/unlabeled or unlabeled/unlabeled images into patches and shuffle them, then enforce consistency.
- Relevance: occupies patch rearrangement and local-context mixing. Public reviews noted possible patch-boundary artifacts and the need for broader validation.
- Gap relative to ViSA-MT: no cross-sample shuffle or artificial boundary; candidate views are selected by validity and learning utility.
- Paper/reviews: https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/586-Paper0942.html
