# SemiGDA

- Paper: *SemiGDA: Generative Dual-distribution Alignment for Semi-Supervised Medical Image Segmentation*.
- Venue/year: CVPR 2026.
- Core idea: distinct image and mask encoders model and align feature/semantic distributions, with consistency-driven skip adapters.
- Relevance: a current top-venue medical SSL comparator and evidence that 2026 novelty expectations extend beyond small augmentation recipes.
- Difference: not an online data augmentation selection method.
- Paper: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Huang_SemiGDA_Generative_Dual-distribution_Alignment_for_Semi-Supervised_Medical_Image_Segmentation_CVPR_2026_paper.html
- Code: https://github.com/taozh2017/SemiGDA
