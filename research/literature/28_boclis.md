# BoCLIS

- Primary source: [IEEE TMI 2025 paper](https://www.isee-ai.cn/~wangruixuan/files/TMI2025Yang.pdf)
- Core: conservative/radical teachers identify boundary regions and enable cross-image
  boundary patch contrastive learning.
- Collision: “learning difficult boundaries” or boundary patch sampling is not novel.
- BMER distinction: input-only evidence resynthesis; no second teacher, patch
  contrastive objective, boundary loss, or inference module.
