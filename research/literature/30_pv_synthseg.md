# PV-SynthSeg

- Primary source: [arXiv:2004.10221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10221)
- Core: synthesizes varying-resolution brain MRI with explicit partial-volume effects
  from high-resolution label maps.
- Collision: physics-inspired resolution/partial-volume augmentation is established.
- Project consequence: stochastic virtual re-acquisition is physically appealing but
  has a denser novelty boundary and weaker current-data validation than BMER.
